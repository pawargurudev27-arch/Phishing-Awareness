Cyber Fraud Defense & Phishing Awareness

Learn how to identify social engineering attacks, protect critical credentials, and respond safely to suspicious messages.

What is Phishing?

📧 Email Phishing[cite: 3]

Fraudulent emails designed to trick users into clicking malicious links or sharing sensitive information.[cite: 1, 3]

📱 Smishing[cite: 3]

Phishing attacks delivered through SMS or messaging applications.[cite: 1, 3]

☎️ Vishing[cite: 3]

Fraudulent phone calls where attackers attempt to obtain confidential information.[cite: 1, 3]

🔗 Fake Websites[cite: 3]

Websites designed to look legitimate and steal usernames, passwords or payment information.[cite: 1, 3]

🚨 Common Warning Signs[cite: 3]

Urgent Requests[cite: 3]

"Your account will be suspended immediately."[cite: 1, 3]

Suspicious Links[cite: 3]

The displayed website address doesn't match the legitimate organization.[cite: 1, 3]

Credential Requests[cite: 3]

Requests for passwords, OTPs or MFA approvals should be treated carefully.[cite: 1, 3]

Unexpected Attachments[cite: 3]

Be cautious with unexpected files or documents.[cite: 1, 3]
