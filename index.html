Cyber Fraud Defense & Phishing Awareness

Learn how to identify social engineering attacks, protect critical credentials, and respond safely to suspicious messages.

What is Phishing?

📧 Email Phishing

Fraudulent emails designed to trick users into clicking malicious links or sharing sensitive information.

📱 Smishing

Phishing attacks delivered through SMS or messaging applications.

☎️ Vishing

Fraudulent phone calls where attackers attempt to obtain confidential information.

🔗 Fake Websites

Websites designed to look legitimate and steal usernames, passwords or payment information.

🚨 Common Warning Signs

Urgent Requests

"Your account will be suspended immediately."

Suspicious Links

The displayed website address doesn't match the legitimate organization.

Credential Requests

Requests for passwords, OTPs or MFA approvals should be treated carefully.

Unexpected Attachments

Be cautious with unexpected files or documents.

🎯 Phishing Email Simulation

Can you identify what's suspicious in this email?

From: security-alert@company-example-security.com
Subject: ⚠️ Urgent: Your account will be suspended
Hello User,

Your corporate account will be suspended today. Please click here to verify your password immediately.

Failure to complete the verification may result in account suspension.

Regards,
IT Security Team

🚨 Suspicious Indicators Found

  • Suspicious sender/domain
  • Urgent/threatening language
  • Request to verify password
  • Link should be verified before clicking

Recommended action: Do not click the link. Verify the request through an official channel and report the suspicious email.

🛡️ What Should You Do?

🛑

STOP

Don't click suspicious links.

🔍

VERIFY

Check the sender and request.

🚨

REPORT

Report suspicious activity.

🗑️

DELETE

Delete the message after reporting.

🔐 Password & MFA Security

Strong Passwords

Use unique passwords for important accounts.

MFA

Never approve an unexpected MFA request.

OTP

Never share OTPs with another person.

Verification

Verify unusual requests through another trusted channel.
